TTS2BluetoothSerial allows you to send Text-to-Speech (TTS) messages directly from your Android device via Bluetooth to ESP32-based, typically ready-made display devices. The app is specifically designed for these navigation devices. It can also process and forward notifications from navigation apps. The app is aimed at bikers and hikers looking for an affordable display device for navigation information. Anyone can easily flash such a device using the available web flasher without any special skills and simply start navigating with the Android app acting as a link to the navigation app. Perfect for navigation prompts or status updates. More information can be found on the project website: https://scriptwriter13.github.io/bikenavi_esp32/
